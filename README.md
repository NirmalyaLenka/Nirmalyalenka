### ■ CORE SYSTEM DEPLOYMENTS

Below is a curated selection of my most complex hardware-software integrations.

> **[ SYS-01 ] : Smart Pressure Ulcer Prevention System**  
> **Stack:** `FPGA` | `Edge AI` | `Capacitive Sensors` | `IoT Telemetry`  
> **Abstract:** A closed-loop medical IoT architecture designed for hospitals. Uses a custom-built dense capacitive sensor mat and FPGA-driven edge inference to monitor bedridden patients, predicting and autonomously mitigating pressure ulcers in real-time.

> **[ SYS-02 ] : 2.4GHz RF Interceptor & Signal Scanner**  
> **Stack:** `ESP32` | `nRF24L01` | `SSD1306 OLED` | `C++`  
> **Abstract:** A custom hardware diagnostics tool that sweeps all 128 channels in the 2.4 GHz band. Intercepts wireless network traffic, visualizes packet-level interference, and maps RF density on a localized hardware display.

> **[ SYS-03 ] : AI Refrigerator Inventory & Vision System**  
> **Stack:** `Computer Vision` | `Raspberry Pi` | `Load Cells` | `Python`  
> **Abstract:** A predictive inventory management system. Integrates camera-based item identification with precision weight sensors to track food supply, predict expiration dates, and automatically generate network-synced shopping lists.

> **[ SYS-04 ] : AccidentGuard Telemetry Module**  
> **Stack:** `ESP32` | `ADXL345 Accelerometer` | `NEO-6M GPS` | `REST APIs`  
> **Abstract:** An autonomous vehicular/wearable safety module. Detects severe impact forces, triangulates real-time spatial coordinates via GPS, and autonomously triggers distress protocols over wireless networks if the user is incapacitated.
